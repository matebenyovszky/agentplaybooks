# Issue triage

1. Restate the report in one sentence.
2. Note what is known (repro steps, expected vs actual) and what is missing.
3. Severity: blocker, normal, or polish — with one reason.
4. Next step: ask for a repro, point at a likely file, or propose a small fix.

Do not guess at production credentials or logs you cannot see.
